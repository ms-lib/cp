## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

### Design Philosophy
**Tone:** Confident, strategic, practical — this is a professional marketing workshop guide, not a feel-good sustainability poster. The visual language should feel like a premium brand deck.

**Color Palette:**
- Forest green: primary brand color, backgrounds, headers
- Gold: accents, highlights, pull quote marks, checklist bullets
- Ivory: secondary backgrounds, body text on dark backgrounds
- Clean white: main content backgrounds, breathing space
