## Page 3 — Context (3 cols)

**Section Header:**
Three Principles of Good Coaching

**Column 1:**
Icon: Ear / listening symbol
Title: Listen
Body: Great coaches listen more than they speak. Give your coachee space to think out loud. Resist the urge to fill silence — often the best insight comes after a pause.

**Column 2:**
Icon: Question mark / dialogue bubble
Title: Ask
Body: Ask open questions that invite reflection, not yes-or-no answers. "What would that look like?" and "What's holding you back?" open doors that advice alone cannot.

**Column 3:**
Icon: Mirror / reflection symbol
Title: Reflect
Body: Help your coachee see patterns they may have missed. Reflect their words back, name what you notice, and invite them to explore what those observations mean for them.

## Page 5 — Core B (3 cols)

**Section Header:**
Three Phases of a Coaching Session

**Column 1:**
Phase: 01
Title: Open
Body: Start with a check-in. Ask how they are arriving — mentally, emotionally. Agree on what they want to focus on today. This sets shared direction and builds trust from the first minute.

**Column 2:**
Phase: 02
Title: Explore
Body: Go deeper into the topic together. Ask questions that challenge assumptions and invite new perspectives. Let the coachee do most of the talking — you are there to expand the conversation, not close it.

**Column 3:**
Phase: 03
Title: Commit
Body: End with clear next steps. Ask: "What will you do before we meet again?" Small, specific commitments create momentum. Celebrate what they discovered — not just what they decided.
