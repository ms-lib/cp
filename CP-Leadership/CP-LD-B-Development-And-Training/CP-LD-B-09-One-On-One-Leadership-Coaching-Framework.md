Three Pillars of Effective 1:1 Coaching

**Column 1:**
Icon: Handshake / lock symbol
Title: Trust
Body: Nothing meaningful happens without it. A 1:1 coaching relationship only works when the leader feels safe to be honest — about struggles, doubts, and goals they haven't voiced to anyone else. Build trust before you build anything else.

**Column 2:**
Icon: Target / crosshairs
Title: Focus
Body: Every session should have a clear purpose. Without focus, conversations drift. With it, both coach and leader leave the session knowing exactly what moved forward. Focused coaching is not rigid — it is respectful of people's time and energy.

**Column 3:**
Icon: Upward arrow / progress chart
Title: Progress
Body: Coaching without progress is just conversation. Track what shifts between sessions — in thinking, behavior, and results. Celebrate small wins. Name what's changing. Progress, made visible, is one of the most powerful motivators a coach can offer.

## Page 5 — Core B (3 cols)

**Section Header:**
Three Phases of a 1:1 Session

**Column 1:**
Phase: 01
Title: Review
Body: Start by looking back. What did the leader commit to last time? What happened? Not to judge, but to learn. This brief reflection builds accountability and surfaces patterns that both coach and leader can work with going forward.

**Column 2:**
Phase: 02
Title: Explore
Body: Move into the main topic of today's session. Ask questions, challenge assumptions, and make space for honest conversation. The coach's job here is not to provide direction — it is to help the leader think more clearly and more boldly.

**Column 3:**
Phase: 03
Title: Plan
Body: Close with commitment. What will the leader do before the next session? Keep it specific and achievable. One clear action is worth more than a long list. End on forward momentum — not reflection, but intention.

**Visual Note:**
