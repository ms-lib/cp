| **Ask Bigger Questions:**<br>Move beyond "how do we fix this?" to "why does this keep happening?" and "what would need to be true for this to go differently?" | **Look for Patterns:**<br>Strategic thinkers connect dots others miss. Regularly step back and ask: what is the same about this situation as the last one? What trend does this reflect? | **Think in Trade-offs:**<br>Every strategic choice involves giving something up. Identify what you are choosing not to do — and make sure that trade-off is a conscious one, not an accident. | **Build for the Future:**<br>Ask what today's decisions make possible or impossible tomorrow. Strategic leaders think about the options they are creating — not just the results they are producing right now. |
