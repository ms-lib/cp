| **Step 1: Know Your Patterns**<br>Identify the situations, people, or pressures that consistently affect your mood or judgment at work. Check in with your team often. Ask how they are doing and actually listen. The more you understand the people you lead — their pressures, their strengths, their concerns — the more effectively and humanely you can lead them. |
