**Jumlah Kolom:** 4  
| Kolom 1 | Kolom 2 | Kolom 3 | Kolom 4 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Step 1: Clarify the Real Question**<br>Many poor decisions start with the wrong question. Pause before deciding. Make sure you are solving the actual problem, not a symptom of it. | **Step 2: Gather What You Need**<br>What information, input, or perspective is missing? Ask the right people. Review what you know. Do not decide in a vacuum — especially on high-stakes issues. | **Step 3: Weigh Your Options Honestly**<br>Consider at least two genuine alternatives. Look at the short and long-term effects. Acknowledge the trade-offs rather than looking for the "perfect" option that does not exist. | **Step 4: Decide and Own It**<br>Make the call. Communicate it clearly. Explain the reasoning when appropriate — and take responsibility for the outcome regardless of where it leads. |
