### PAGE 5 - CORE CONTENT B
**Fase:** CORE CONTENT  
**Tipe Halaman:** List/Value  
**Judul Halaman:** The Four-Step Decision Framework  
**Jumlah Kolom:** 4  
| Kolom 1 | Kolom 2 | Kolom 3 | Kolom 4 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Step 1: Define**<br>What decision actually needs to be made? Name it clearly. A well-defined decision is already halfway solved. Remove assumptions before moving forward. | **Step 2: Assess**<br>What do you know? What do you not know? Identify the key information, consult the right people, and map out the real options — not just the obvious first choice. | **Step 3: Decide**<br>Apply your values, priorities, and the available information. Choose the option that best serves the team and the organization — not just the most comfortable path. | **Step 4: Communicate**<br>Share the decision clearly. Explain your reasoning to the degree appropriate. Give the team what they need to act — and follow through on what you have committed to. |

**Visual Note:** Four numbered framework step cards in a horizontal flow. Bold gold numbers, navy card backgrounds, ivory text. Clean and modern.
