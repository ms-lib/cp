### PAGE 5 - CORE CONTENT B
**Fase:** CORE CONTENT  
**Tipe Halaman:** List/Value  
**Judul Halaman:** Three Listening Practices For Leaders  
**Jumlah Kolom:** 3  
| Kolom 1 | Kolom 2 | Kolom 3 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Listen Without Interrupting:**<br>Let the person finish. Fully. Resist the urge to finish their sentence, offer a solution, or redirect the conversation. The simple act of letting someone speak without interruption sends a powerful signal: you matter, and I am here. | **Reflect Back What You Heard:**<br>Before responding, summarize what you understood. "It sounds like you're feeling..." or "What I'm hearing is..." gives the speaker a chance to confirm or correct — and shows that you were genuinely paying attention. | **Ask One More Question:**<br>After someone shares, ask one follow-up question before offering your view. "Can you tell me more about that?" or "What's been the hardest part?" deepens the conversation and often reveals what the person most needed to say but had not yet. |

**Visual Note:** Three bold listening practice cards in slate and rose. Warm, clean design. Icon per practice.
