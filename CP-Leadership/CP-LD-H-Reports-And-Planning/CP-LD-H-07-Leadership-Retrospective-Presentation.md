### PAGE 5 - CORE B

**Tipe Halaman:** Core B  
**Judul Halaman:** Three Retrospective Phases  
**Jumlah Kolom:** 3  
| Kolom 1 | Kolom 2 | Kolom 3 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Look Back**<br><br>Before evaluating what happened, the team needs to see the year clearly. What were the key moments? What were the decisions that shaped outcomes? What were the moments that tested us? This phase is about building a shared picture of the year before drawing conclusions about it. | **Evaluate Honestly**<br><br>With a shared picture in place, the team asks the hard questions. What in that picture reflects strong leadership? What reflects choices we'd make differently today? This phase works only when every voice in the room is heard and no one's observation is dismissed. | **Commit to Action**<br><br>The retrospective ends with decisions, not observations. The team chooses the top two or three changes to carry forward. These commitments are documented, assigned to owners, and revisited at the next review cycle. Without this phase, reflection is just storytelling. |
