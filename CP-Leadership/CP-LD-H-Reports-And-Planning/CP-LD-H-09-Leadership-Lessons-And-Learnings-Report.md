### PAGE 5 - CORE B

**Tipe Halaman:** Core B
**Judul Halaman:** Three Key Lessons From This Period
**Jumlah Kolom:** 3

| Kolom 1 | Kolom 2 | Kolom 3 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Clarity Prevents Most Problems**<br><br>The most common source of friction this year was unclear expectations — about roles, about priorities, about what success actually looked like. When leaders took the time to be specific upfront, teams moved faster and produced better work. The lesson: invest in clarity before the work begins, not after the confusion appears. | **Trust Requires Consistency**<br><br>Trust is built in repeated small actions — following through on commitments, being present in difficult conversations, and behaving the same way whether or not anyone is watching. This year reminded us that trust is fragile when leaders are inconsistent and powerful when they are not. The lesson: the small things are never small. | **Learning Needs Space**<br><br>When teams are moving too fast to reflect, they stop learning from what they do. This year, the teams that built in brief review moments — even just a short debrief after key milestones — made better decisions in the next phase of work. The lesson: reflection is not a luxury. It is part of how good teams get better. |
