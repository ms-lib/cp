## Page 3 — Context (3 Columns)

**Section Title:**
Three Dimensions of Leadership Impact

**Column 1 — Culture**
**Column Heading:** Culture

Leadership shapes how people feel about their work. This year, our leaders took visible steps to build psychological safety, model honest communication, and create environments where people wanted to contribute. Culture moved from a value we talked about to a standard we held ourselves to.

**Column 2 — Performance**
**Column Heading:** Performance

Clear expectations and strong accountability drove results. Our leaders set focused goals, reviewed progress consistently, and supported their teams in removing obstacles. The outcome was a more disciplined, high-output organization that kept moving forward without burning people out.

**Column 3 — Growth**
**Column Heading:** Growth

We grew this year — in reach, in capability, and in confidence. Leadership opened new opportunities for individuals and teams, created pathways for promotion, and expanded the ways we serve our stakeholders. Growth was not just measured in numbers but in the people who stepped into bigger roles.
