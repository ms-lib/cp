## Page 3 — Context (2 Columns)

**Section Title:**
Two Dimensions of Leadership Performance

**Column 1 — What Leaders Deliver**
**Column Heading:** What Leaders Deliver

This dimension looks at outcomes: goals achieved, projects delivered, and measurable results that show whether leadership translated vision into action. It asks: did this leader move the organization forward in ways that can be seen and measured?

**Column 2 — How They Lead**
**Column Heading:** How They Lead

This dimension looks at approach: how leaders communicate, how they build trust, how they develop others, and how they behave under pressure. It asks: is this leader creating an environment where people can do their best work?

Why This Assessment Matters

**Body Paragraph:**
Leadership performance affects every person in the organization. When leaders are clear, confident, and accountable, their teams perform better and feel more engaged. This assessment exists to give leaders an honest picture of where they stand — and a clear path to where they can go next.

## Page 5 — Core B (4 Columns)

**Section Title:**
Four Areas Assessed

**Column 1**
**Label:** Goal Achievement
**Description:** Did the leader set clear goals and follow through? Were targets met, adjusted wisely, and communicated openly to the team?

**Column 2**
**Label:** Team Development
**Description:** Did the leader invest in others? Were people coached, supported, and given room to grow into greater responsibility?

**Column 3**
**Label:** Decision Quality
**Description:** Did the leader make sound decisions in a timely way? Were choices informed by data, shaped by context, and explained clearly?

**Column 4**
**Label:** Communication
**Description:** Did the leader communicate consistently and clearly? Did team members know where they stood and where the organization was heading?

## Page 8 — Emotional Peak (1 Column)

**Section Title:**
Honest Assessment Is an Act of Respect

**Body Paragraph:**
To assess someone's leadership honestly is to take them seriously. It says: we believe you can grow, we respect you enough to tell you the truth, and we are committed to supporting what comes next. Assessment without that intention is just evaluation. Done with care, it is one of the most powerful investments an organization can make in its people.

## Page 9 — Closing (2 Columns)

**Section Title:**
What Comes After the Assessment

**Column 1**
**Heading:** For Individual Leaders
Each leader receives a personal debrief with a clear summary of findings, three areas of strength to build on, and two priority areas for development. A 90-day action plan is created in collaboration with each leader's manager or coach.

**Column 2**
**Heading:** For the Organization
Aggregate findings from this assessment cycle will shape our next leadership development investment, inform succession planning, and help us identify where organizational support is needed most. Results will be shared in a summary report with senior leadership.
