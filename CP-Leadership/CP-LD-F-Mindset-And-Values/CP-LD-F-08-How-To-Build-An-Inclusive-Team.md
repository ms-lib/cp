|---------|---------|---------|
| **Make Room in Meetings:**<br>Structure your meetings so that different people have the floor — not just the most confident voices. Call on quieter team members directly. Rotate who opens the discussion. Rotate who leads the summary. | **Give Feedback Equitably:**<br>Check that you are giving growth opportunities and challenging feedback to every team member — not just the ones you find easiest to talk to. Equity of feedback is equity of opportunity. | **Celebrate What Is Different:**<br>When team members approach problems differently, name that as a strength. "That is a perspective I had not considered" is one of the most powerful sentences an inclusive leader can say. |
