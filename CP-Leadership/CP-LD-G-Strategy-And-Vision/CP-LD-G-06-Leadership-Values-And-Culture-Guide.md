## Page 3 — Context (2 Columns)

**Headline:** Two Sides Of Leadership Culture

**Column 1**
**Values We Hold**
At Liceria & Co., our leadership culture begins with what we believe. We hold values that guide how we make decisions, how we treat people, and what we stand for when things get hard. These are not words on a wall — they are the standards every leader is expected to carry and protect, regardless of role, team, or situation.

**Column 2**
**Behaviors We Model**
Values only matter when they show up in behavior. We ask every leader at Liceria & Co. to model the culture visibly and consistently — not just in formal settings, but in the small daily moments where real culture is built. Behavior is how values become real for the people around us.

**Body:**
Values are only real when they shape real decisions — when a leader chooses integrity over convenience. At Liceria & Co., values must be named, practiced, and held to account. When leaders hold each other accountable, culture stops being a statement and starts being how the organization works.

## Page 5 — Core B (4 Columns)

**Headline:** Four Guide Principles

**Column 1**
**Principle 01 — Lead With Integrity**
Do what you say. Hold to your values even when it is difficult. The team trusts leaders who are consistent — not leaders who say the right things and do different ones.

**Column 2**
**Principle 02 — Respect Every Person**
Treat every team member with dignity, regardless of role or rank. A culture of respect starts with the leader and spreads through the whole organization when it is modeled with consistency and sincerity.

**Column 3**
**Principle 03 — Speak Honestly**
Say what is true, clearly and with care. Honest leaders build trusting teams. Create a culture where honest conversations are normal — not feared — and the whole organization becomes stronger for it.

**Column 4**
**Principle 04 — Take Responsibility**
Own your outcomes — both the successes and the mistakes. Leaders who take responsibility without blame build teams that feel safe to try, fail, and grow. That safety is the foundation of a learning culture.
