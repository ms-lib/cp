## Page 3 — Context (3 Columns)

**Headline:** Three Components Of A Leadership Pipeline

**Column 1**
**Talent Identification**
A strong pipeline starts with seeing clearly who has the potential to lead at the next level. Talent identification is a deliberate process — looking beyond current performance to find team members who have the mindset, values, and drive to grow into senior leadership roles.

**Column 2**
**Structured Development**
Potential alone does not create leaders. The pipeline must include deliberate development — coaching, mentorship, cross-functional exposure, and real leadership opportunities that build the skills and confidence future leaders will need when they step into greater responsibility.

**Column 3**
**Transition Readiness**
The pipeline delivers results at the moment of transition. Leaders in the pipeline must be actively prepared for the specific roles they are moving toward — not just developed in general. Readiness means the organization can move quickly and with confidence when a leadership role opens.

## Page 5 — Core B (3 Columns)

**Headline:** Three Program Stages

**Column 1**
**Stage 1 — Build the Foundation**
Identify high-potential leaders across the organization. Map critical roles that will need succession within the next one to three years. Set clear criteria for what readiness looks like — so selection is consistent, fair, and aligned with the organization's direction.

**Column 2**
**Stage 2 — Develop for the Role**
Create individual development plans tied to specific leadership roles. Combine formal learning with on-the-job experience, mentorship from senior leaders, and regular coaching checkpoints that keep development on track and responsive to the leader's actual growth.

**Column 3**
**Stage 3 — Manage the Handover**
When the transition arrives, activate the plan. Support both the outgoing and incoming leader through the handover. Communicate clearly to the team. Measure the success of the transition and use what you learn to strengthen the pipeline for the next cycle.
