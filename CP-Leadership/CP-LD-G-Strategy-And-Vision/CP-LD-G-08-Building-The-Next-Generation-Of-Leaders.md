## Page 3 — Context (3 Columns)

**Headline:** Three Things Next-Generation Leaders Need Most

**Column 1**
**Real Experience**
Next-generation leaders do not grow in classrooms alone. They grow through real challenges — stretch assignments, cross-team projects, and moments where they have to make real decisions with real consequences. Experience is where leadership becomes real.

**Column 2**
**Honest Feedback**
Future leaders need to know exactly where they stand — what they do well and where they need to grow. Honest, caring feedback given early and regularly is one of the most powerful investments an organization can make in the leaders of tomorrow.

**Column 3**
**Trusted Mentors**
The fastest way to grow a leader is to put them alongside a great one. Mentorship gives emerging leaders access to thinking, experience, and perspective they cannot get any other way — and it builds the relationships that sustain great leadership across generations.

## Page 5 — Core B (3 Columns)

**Headline:** Three Ways Organizations Build Future Leaders

**Column 1**
**Stretch Them Early**
Give high-potential team members responsibilities that push them beyond their comfort zone. The discomfort of a stretch assignment is where the most important growth happens — and it shows emerging leaders that the organization believes in them before they feel fully ready.

**Column 2**
**Connect Them to Senior Leaders**
Create formal mentorship and sponsorship programs that put emerging leaders in direct contact with senior decision-makers. The relationships built in these programs often shape the trajectory of an entire career — and strengthen the whole organization.

**Column 3**
**Give Them Visible Projects**
Assign future leaders to work that matters and is seen. Visibility builds confidence, creates accountability, and gives emerging leaders a track record that the broader organization can trust when it is time to move them into greater responsibility.
