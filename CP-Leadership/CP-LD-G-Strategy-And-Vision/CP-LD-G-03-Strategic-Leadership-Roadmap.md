## Page 3 — Context (3 Columns)

**Headline:** Three Components Of A Strategic Leadership Roadmap

**Column 1**
**Clear Priorities**
A roadmap only works when it names what matters most. Clear priorities help leaders say no to distractions and yes to the work that actually moves the organization in the right direction.

**Column 2**
**Defined Milestones**
Every roadmap needs markers — moments where the team can measure progress, celebrate movement, and adjust if needed. Milestones keep the journey visible and the team motivated.

**Column 3**
**Shared Ownership**
The best roadmaps belong to the whole team. When every person understands their role in the plan, they stop waiting for direction and start taking initiative toward the agreed destination.

## Page 4 — Core A (1 Column)

**Headline:** Why Leaders Need A Roadmap, Not Just A Plan

**Body:**
A plan tells you what to do. A roadmap tells you where you are going — and how far you have come. Teams with a shared roadmap move faster, communicate better, and recover from setbacks with more confidence than those working from a plan alone.

---

## Page 5 — Core B (4 Columns)

**Headline:** Four Roadmap Phases

**Column 1**
**Phase 01 — Assess**
Start by looking honestly at where you are. What is working, what is not, and what the team needs most right now. Good strategy always begins with clear-eyed assessment — not assumptions.

**Column 2**
**Phase 02 — Plan**
Define your priorities, set your milestones, and assign ownership. A good plan is simple enough to remember and specific enough to act on. Build in flexibility so the team can adapt without losing direction.

**Column 3**
**Phase 03 — Execute**
Move from planning to doing. Keep the roadmap visible, check in regularly, and remove obstacles that slow the team down. Execution is where vision becomes real.

**Column 4**
**Phase 04 — Review**
Step back and measure progress honestly. Celebrate what worked, learn from what did not, and update the roadmap to reflect what the team has learned. A roadmap that never changes is not being used.
