## Page 3 — Context (2 Columns)

**Headline:** Two Foundations Of Leadership Culture

**Column 1**
**Behaviors**
Culture is built by what leaders do every day — how they communicate, how they treat people, and how they respond under pressure. Consistent leadership behaviors, practiced across the organization, become the culture that everyone else learns from and models. When behaviors are strong, culture follows without having to be forced.

**Column 2**
**Systems**
Behaviors need structure to sustain them. Strong leadership culture is supported by systems — how leaders are developed, recognized, and held accountable. When the organization builds systems that reinforce the right behaviors, culture becomes self-sustaining and resilient across teams, time, and change.

## Page 4 — Core A (1 Column)

**Headline:** Culture Starts At The Top

**Body:**
Every organization has a leadership culture — designed or not. What the leaders at the top model becomes the standard everyone else follows. If the culture feels off, look at the top first — because change that starts there moves through the whole organization with speed and conviction.

## Page 5 — Core B (4 Columns)

**Headline:** Four Steps To Build Leadership Culture

**Column 1**
**Step 01 — Name It**
Be specific. Name the behaviors, values, and standards you want to see. Vague culture goals produce vague results. Name exactly what good leadership looks like at Liceria & Co.

**Column 2**
**Step 02 — Model It**
Senior leaders must go first. The team will follow what they see — not what they are told. When the top team models the culture clearly and consistently, everyone else has a real example to follow.

**Column 3**
**Step 03 — Build It In**
Embed the culture into how you develop, reward, and review leaders. When culture is built into your systems, it does not depend on individual effort to survive. It becomes the default way of working.

**Column 4**
**Step 04 — Reinforce It**
Call it out when you see it. Recognize leaders who model the culture well. Address it when the culture slips. Cultures that are talked about, celebrated, and corrected openly are the ones that stay strong over time.
