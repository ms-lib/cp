## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Color palette:** Navy (#1A2744), gold (#C9A84C), ivory (#FAF7F0), clean white (#FFFFFF)
- **Page 1 (Cover):** Full-bleed navy background with gold headline. Subheadline in ivory. Consider a bold horizontal gold rule as a design accent.
- **Page 2 (Opener):** Label field names in gold, values in dark body color. Clean single-column layout with generous margins.
- **Page 3 (Context):** Three equal columns. Each column has a bold title in navy or gold and body in standard body weight. A top accent line or icon per column adds polish.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Section label small and spaced above the main headline. Body paragraph in regular weight below.
- **Page 5 (Core B):** Three columns matching Page 3 visual treatment. Section title centered and prominent above all three columns.
- **Page 6 (Core C):** Two-column layout. Each column: name bold, title in gold italic, quote in a slightly larger italic, bio in regular body size. Consider a thin vertical rule between columns.
- **Page 7 (Engagement):** Four equal cards in a grid. Card header: name in bold, title in gold. Description in smaller body text. Cards can have a navy border or subtle gold top accent.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Pull quote large and centered — consider a large opening quotation mark as a graphic element. Attribution in smaller gold text. Body paragraph below in regular weight.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Two-column layout. One column can use a navy or light ivory background panel for contrast.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Closing line in large bold gold. Organization name prominent. Contact placeholder small at bottom.
- **Typography:** Suggest a strong sans-serif for headlines (e.g., Montserrat Bold) and clean body text (e.g., Source Sans Pro or Inter).
- **60% rule:** All text boxes should leave 40% of their area as white space — especially in body-heavy sections.
