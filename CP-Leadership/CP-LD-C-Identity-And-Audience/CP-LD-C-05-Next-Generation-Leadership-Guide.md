## Page 5 — Core B (4 cols)

**Section Label:** Four Guide Principles

**Col 1:**
Principle 01
**Know Yourself First**
Self-awareness is the starting point. Understand how you think, how you respond under pressure, and how others experience you. Leadership begins from the inside out.

**Col 2:**
Principle 02
**Communicate With Clarity**
Leaders speak so people understand. Practice saying complex ideas simply. Listen as much — or more — than you speak. Clarity builds credibility.

**Col 3:**
Principle 03
**Make Decisions Boldly**
Indecision is a decision too. Learn to gather what you need, move with confidence, and own the outcome — whether it goes well or not.

**Col 4:**
Principle 04
**Keep Growing Deliberately**
The best leaders never stop learning. Seek feedback, read widely, reflect often, and stay open to being wrong. Growth is not automatic — it takes intention.

## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Color Palette:** Navy, teal, ivory, clean white
- **Page 1 (Cover):** Navy background with teal and ivory typography. Bold, structured, and professional. The tone is confident — not loud.
- **Page 2 (Opener):** Label fields styled cleanly in teal on ivory or white. No "The" prefix on labels in this file — all labels are consistent without it.
- **Page 3 (2 cols):** Two equal columns with strong visual contrast. Navy block for Character, teal block for Capability. Highlight tags styled as small bold labels.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Pull quote centered and large. Navy or teal background option for the quote block. Strong typographic hierarchy.
- **Page 5 (4 cols):** Principle numbers oversized and bold. Consistent column width. Clean dividers or white space between columns.
- **Page 6 (3 cols):** Circular photo placeholder per profile. Name and role in bold above quote. Quote in italic. Bio in standard body weight. Keep consistent visual treatment across all 3 columns.
- **Page 7 (2 cols):** Testimonial layout with large quotation marks as design element. Names in short-form: first name + last initial. Differentiated background from adjacent pages.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Navy full-bleed option. Headline large and centered. Closing line isolated and bold.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Clear two-column separation. Logistics on left, audience list on right. Checkmark or dot icons for the list.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Minimal layout. Tagline as primary visual focus. Navy or teal color treatment to close the visual arc.
- **Typography:** Bold geometric sans-serif for headlines. Clean sans-serif for body. Italics for quotes.
- **Imagery:** Photo placeholder boxes on Page 6. Optional texture or pattern on Page 1 for depth.
