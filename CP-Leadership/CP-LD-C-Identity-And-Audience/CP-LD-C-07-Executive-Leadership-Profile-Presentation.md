## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Color palette:** Deep slate (#2C3345), gold (#C9A84C), ivory (#FAF7F0), clean white (#FFFFFF)
- **Page 1 (Cover):** Full-bleed deep slate background with gold headline. Liceria & Co. name in ivory. Use gold rule or line element beneath headline.
- **Page 2 (Opener):** Label fields in gold or slate. Body text in dark body color. Clean and minimal — allow generous white space.
- **Page 3 (Context):** Three equal columns with gold icon or number above each column title. Column titles bold in slate or gold.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Single-column with strong headline. Consider a full-width horizontal rule above the section label.
- **Page 5 (Core B):** Three equal columns matching Page 3 treatment. Section title centered above all columns.
- **Page 6 (Core C):** Two-column spotlight. Left column for lead voice, right for supporting voice. Each column has name, title in gold, quote in italics, and short bio.
- **Page 7 (Engagement):** Four equal role cards. Each card: name in bold, title in gold, short description. Can use card/box layout with slate border or gold accent.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Large pull quote centered — set in an elegant serif or large italic. Attribution below in smaller text. Body paragraph below in regular weight.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Two-column layout. Consider using a light slate or ivory background for one column as contrast.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Closing line in gold or large bold. Organization name prominent. Contact placeholder at bottom in smaller type.
- **Typography:** Suggest a classic serif for headlines (e.g., Playfair Display) and clean sans-serif for body (e.g., Lato or Inter).
- **60% rule:** All text boxes should leave 40% of their area as white space to give the layout room to breathe.
