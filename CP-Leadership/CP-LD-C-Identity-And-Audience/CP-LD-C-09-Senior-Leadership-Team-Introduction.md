## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Color palette:** Slate (#3D4F5C), teal (#2A7F7F), ivory (#FAF7F0), clean white (#FFFFFF)
- **Page 1 (Cover):** Full-bleed slate background with teal or ivory headline. Subheadline in ivory. A teal horizontal accent bar or rule adds vibrancy.
- **Page 2 (Opener):** Label field names in teal, values in dark body color. Single-column with wide margins and generous line spacing.
- **Page 3 (Context):** Three columns with teal accent on column titles. A top icon or number mark above each heading adds visual structure. Use clean white or ivory background.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Section label in teal above bold headline. Body paragraph in regular weight below with comfortable line height.
- **Page 5 (Core B):** Three columns. Section title above centered in bold slate. Each column title in teal bold. Body in regular weight.
- **Page 6 (Core C):** Two-column spotlight. Name bold, title in teal, quote in italic, bio in regular. Consider a subtle teal top border or accent line on each column block.
- **Page 7 (Engagement):** Four equal cards in a grid. Name bold, title in teal, short description in body size. Clean card borders in slate or teal.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Pull quote large and centered — can use a large teal opening quote mark as a graphic detail. Attribution in smaller teal or slate. Body paragraph below.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Two-column layout. Consider an ivory or teal-tinted background on one column for contrast and warmth.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Full-bleed slate or teal background. Closing line large and bold in ivory or white. Organization name prominent. Contact placeholder at bottom in smaller lighter text.
- **Typography:** Suggest a clean, modern sans-serif for all text (e.g., Inter, DM Sans, or Nunito). Vary weights for hierarchy — bold for names and headlines, regular for body.
- **60% rule:** Keep all text boxes to 60% fill maximum — especially important on card pages and the opener to preserve the clean, open feel.
