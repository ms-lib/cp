## NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Color Palette:** Teal, gold, ivory, clean white
- **Page 1 (Cover):** Bold headline in teal on clean white or ivory background. Gold accent on supporting line. High-energy feel — use strong typography.
- **Page 2 (Opener):** Single column, label fields styled with gold or teal. Keep generous white space.
- **Page 3 (2 cols):** Clear visual divide between the two columns. Use contrasting color blocks — teal for Skills, gold for Confidence. Short highlights styled as callout badges.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Pull quote should be large, centered, and styled in teal or gold with italic weight.
- **Page 5 (4 cols):** Pillar numbers (01–04) styled bold and oversized. Equal column width. Icons optional per pillar.
- **Page 6 (3 cols):** Profile photo placeholder per column (circular crop recommended). Name and role clearly above quote. Keep quote in italic. Bio in smaller body text.
- **Page 7 (2 cols):** Testimonial quotes styled with large quotation marks in teal. Names in short-form: first name + last initial only. Light background to differentiate from surrounding pages.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Full-bleed teal or gold background option. Headline large and centered. Closing line bold and standalone.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Two-column layout. Left col for logistical info, right col for bullet audience list. Use icons or checkmarks for the bullet list.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Centered layout. Tagline as the hero text. Clean and minimal — mirror the energy of the cover.
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif for headlines. Clean sans-serif for body. Italics for quotes only.
- **Imagery:** Placeholder image boxes for Page 6 (profile photos) and optional background image on Page 8.
