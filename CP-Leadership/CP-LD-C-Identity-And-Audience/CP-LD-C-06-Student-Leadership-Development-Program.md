**Col 3:**

- **Page 3 (2 cols):** Two equal columns. Slate block for Personal Leadership, amber block for Team Leadership. Highlight tags as small, bold callout lines at the bottom of each column.
- **Page 4 (Core A):** Headline large and confident. Pull quote centered, italic, in slate or amber. Consider a subtle divider or block background for the quote.
- **Page 5 (4 cols):** Phase numbers oversized. Consistent column width. Slate or amber phase number accents. Icons optional per phase.
- **Page 6 (3 cols):** Circular photo placeholder per profile. Name and role bold above quote. Quote in italic. Bio in smaller standard weight. All three columns visually consistent.
- **Page 7 (2 cols):** Testimonial layout with oversized quotation marks as visual anchors. Names in short-form: first name + last initial. Differentiated from surrounding pages by background color or texture.
- **Page 8 (Emotional Peak):** Slate full-bleed option. Large, centered headline. Closing line styled as a standalone element — italic or bold, set apart with whitespace.
- **Page 9 (Closing):** Two-column split. Left column for application logistics (dates, location). Right column for bullet audience list. Use checkmarks or dots for list styling.
- **Page 10 (Back Cover):** Minimal. Tagline as the hero. Slate or amber accent. Mirrors the visual tone of the cover to close the arc cleanly.
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif for headlines. Clean sans-serif for body. Italics for quotes only.
- **Imagery:** Photo placeholder boxes on Page 6. Optional abstract pattern or texture on Page 1 and Page 8 for depth.
